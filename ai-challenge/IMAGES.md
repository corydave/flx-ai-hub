The website looks for every picture in the `images/` folder. **Two pictures are still missing** (marked ☐ below): `what-is-ai-perplexity-logo.png` and `notebooklm-change-title.png`. Until they are saved under those names, they show as broken images on the page.

## How to download

- **Google Drawings** (addresses starting `https://docs.google.com/drawings/...`): open the drawing in Google Drive and choose **File → Download → PNG image (.png)**. Or open the address below in your browser, right-click the picture, and choose **Save image as**.
- **Google Drive files** (addresses starting `https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/...`): open the address below in your browser, right-click the picture, and choose **Save image as**. These addresses have had their size limit removed, so you get the full-size picture.
- **Save each file into `ai-challenge/images/` with exactly the name shown.** Names are lowercase, with hyphens instead of spaces.
- **Every name ends in `.png`.** If a picture downloads as `.jpg` or `.webp`, either convert it to PNG or save it as it is and change its name in `index.html` to match.
- **Card pictures are 400 × 300 pixels.** Other pictures can be any size; the page shrinks them to fit.

Tick each one off as you go.

## Checking your work

Open `index.html` in your browser. Every card should show its picture, and every challenge should show its pictures with no broken-image icons. If one is broken, check that the file name matches the table exactly, including the ending (`.png`, `.gif`, or `.svg`).

## Adding new pictures later

Save the picture in `images/` and use its name in `index.html`, for example `<img src="images/my-picture.png" alt="What the picture shows">`. Pictures linked straight from other websites are blocked by the page's security settings, so always save a copy in `images/`.
