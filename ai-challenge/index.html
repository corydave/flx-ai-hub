Privacy and embedded content

  • This website does not collect information about you. It does not use cookies, analytics, sign-ins, or tracking, and it does not record anything you type into it.
  • Some challenges show content from other companies. These include YouTube videos, a Spotify episode, Google Drive and Google Docs files, a Google Apps Script tool, and a demo hosted on a personal GitHub Pages site. That content loads only when you open a challenge that contains it. When it loads, your browser contacts that company directly, and it may set cookies or record your visit under its own privacy policy.
  • Styling and fonts come from outside services. Bootstrap and Font Awesome are loaded from cdnjs.cloudflare.com, fonts from Google Fonts, and the challenge text styling from aichallenges.org. Each of those services can see your internet address, as any website's host can.
  • The AI tools linked in the challenges are run by their own companies. Once you leave this website, their terms and privacy policies apply.
  • Think before you share. Do not paste personal, student, employee, or confidential information into an AI tool unless your organization allows it.
